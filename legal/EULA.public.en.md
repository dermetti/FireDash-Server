# IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE — BETA SOFTWARE

FireDash is in the development and testing phase. The Software may contain known or unknown bugs, security vulnerabilities, incomplete functionality and other defects. It may fail partially or completely without warning at any time or display incorrect, incomplete or outdated information, including during ongoing emergency operations.

**Errors or failures may contribute to incorrect decisions, operational delays, mission failure, property damage, financial loss, serious injury or death.**

FireDash is intended exclusively to supplement independently functioning processes. It does not replace professional judgment, binding operational procedures, authoritative information sources or required safety-critical systems.

**Safe operational capability must not depend on FireDash. Suitable, independent, available within the required operational time, documented and regularly tested fallback solutions must be maintained for every function used operationally.**

No voluntary guarantees are provided, particularly concerning availability, accuracy, security, reliability or mission success. Liability is governed by the applicable license, this Agreement and the law. Mandatory statutory rights and liabilities remain unaffected.

## § 6 Ability to operate independently of FireDash and fallback solutions

(1) The Operating Organization must ensure that safe and proper performance of its tasks does not depend on the availability, accuracy, completeness or correct functioning of FireDash.

(2) Suitable Fallback Solutions must be established, maintained, documented and regularly tested for every function used for organizational or emergency-service operations. Tasks must be capable of continuing without unacceptable impairment even in the event of partial or complete unavailability, malfunction or loss of trustworthiness.

(3) Fallback Solutions must be independent to a degree appropriate to the risks from FireDash Server and clients, server, storage and database infrastructure, end devices, network and internet connections, external services and potentially incorrect or manipulated information and datasets.

(4) Particular consideration must be given to technical failures, incorrect or outdated information, synchronization delays, device loss, power and communications outages, data loss, corruption, unauthorized changes, cyberattacks and malfunctions that are not immediately apparent.

(5) Before first operational use, the Operating Organization must test all intended functions and Fallback Solutions under appropriate realistic conditions, document their suitability and approve them internally for operational use. Installation for testing and evaluation remains possible. Internal approval does not constitute acceptance of contracted work performed by the Provider.

(6) Following material changes to Software, infrastructure, information or workflows, affected functions and Fallback Solutions must be reassessed appropriately. The organization determines regular testing intervals according to its own risk assessment, the importance of the function and statutory and organizational requirements.

(7) Authorized Users must be informed about Fallback Solutions and appropriately instructed. The solutions must actually be available and usable within the time required for the relevant task.

(8) Technical data backups alone are insufficient if they do not enable tasks to continue in time.

(9) The requirements for the ability to operate independently of FireDash apply to operational use covered by this Agreement irrespective of the development status of the software version in use, including regular releases after beta. FireDash must not constitute a single point of failure whose failure prevents the safe performance of the relevant tasks.

(10) As part of its risk assessment, the Operating Organization must establish appropriate procedures for detecting operationally or safety-relevant malfunctions, conflicting information and insufficiently up-to-date data, as well as appropriate criteria for switching to Fallback Solutions. If safety-relevant doubts arise about the accuracy, up-to-dateness, completeness or trustworthiness of information provided by FireDash, that information must be independently verified or replaced with suitable alternative information sources, taking account of the operational situation and applicable professional procedures.

(11) The Provider’s statutory responsibilities and statutory rules on causation, attribution and contributory negligence remain unaffected.
